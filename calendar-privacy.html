Privacy policy: Personal Calendar Helper

This tool is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed: the owner's Google Calendar list, events and free/busy information, with the owner's consent.

Use: only to carry out the owner's own requests. Not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and sharing: stored only on devices the owner controls. Not sold or shared with third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
